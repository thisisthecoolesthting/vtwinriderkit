How to Install a Motorcycle Phone Mount Safely and Securely

How to Install a Motorcycle Phone Mount Safely and Securely

A reliable motorcycle phone mount is essential for navigation, tracking rides, or answering calls while keeping your hands on the handlebars. However, improper installation can lead to a dropped phone, distractions, or even damage to your bike. Whether you ride a V-twin cruiser or a high-speed sportbike, securing your phone correctly ensures safety and convenience. Here’s a step-by-step guide to installing a motorcycle phone mount the right way.

Types of Motorcycle Phone Mounts

Before installation, choose the right mount for your bike and riding style. The most common types include:

  1. Handlebar Mounts – Clamp directly onto the handlebars, ideal for most V-twins and sportbikes. Look for adjustable clamps to fit different bar diameters.
  2. Stem Mounts – Attach to the steering stem nut (common on sportbikes). These offer a centered, low-profile position.
  3. Clutch/Brake Reservoir Mounts – Secure to the brake or clutch fluid reservoir, best for bikes with limited handlebar space.
  4. RAM Mounts (Ball-and-Socket Systems) – Highly adjustable with rubberized grips, suitable for rough terrain or high speeds.
  5. Magnetic or Quick-Release Mounts – Convenient for frequent removal but may lack vibration damping.

What to Look for in a Motorcycle Phone Mount

  • Secure Attachment Mechanism – Rubberized grips, strong clamps, or locking mechanisms prevent slippage.
  • Vibration Damping – Look for silicone pads or shock-absorbing designs to protect your phone’s camera and internals.
  • Weather Resistance – Waterproof or water-resistant materials ensure durability in rain.
  • Compatibility – Verify the mount fits your phone’s size and your bike’s mounting points.
  • Adjustability – Swivel or tilt features help optimize visibility while riding.

Step-by-Step Installation Guide

1. Choose the Mounting Location

For handlebar mounts, select a spot that doesn’t interfere with controls or cables. On sportbikes, the stem mount may offer better visibility. For V-twins, handlebar mounts often work best due to wider bars.

2. Prepare the Mounting Surface

Clean the area (handlebar, stem, or reservoir) with alcohol wipes to remove grease or dirt. This ensures a firm grip and prevents slippage.

3. Attach the Mounting Base

  • Handlebar Mounts: Open the clamp, position it around the bar, and tighten the screws evenly. Avoid overtightening, which can damage the bar or mount.
  • Stem Mounts: Remove the steering stem nut, place the mount, and re-secure the nut with the mount in place. Use thread locker to prevent loosening.
  • Reservoir Mounts: Secure the bracket around the reservoir with straps or adhesive pads, ensuring it doesn’t obstruct access to the reservoir cap.

4. Secure the Phone Holder

Attach the phone cradle or grip mechanism to the base. For RAM mounts, connect the ball arm to the base and tighten the socket. For magnetic mounts, ensure the adhesive plate is firmly stuck to your phone or case.

5. Test for Stability

Before riding:

  • Shake the mount firmly to check for movement.
  • Ensure your phone doesn’t obstruct the speedometer, indicators, or controls.
  • Verify the mount doesn’t vibrate excessively at idle.

6. Add Extra Security (Optional)

  • Use a secondary tether (like a lanyard) for added safety on rough roads.
  • Apply vibration dampeners (silicone pads) if your mount doesn’t include them.

Maintenance and Safety Tips

  • Regularly Inspect the Mount: Check screws and clamps for loosening, especially after long rides.
  • Avoid Overloading: Some mounts aren’t designed for heavy phones with thick cases—verify weight limits.
  • Weatherproof Your Phone: If the mount isn’t fully waterproof, use a waterproof phone case or bag.
  • Position for Minimal Distraction: Angle the phone so you can glance at it without taking your eyes off the road for long.

Final Thoughts

A well-installed phone mount keeps your device secure while enhancing ride convenience. Choose a mount that suits your bike’s setup, follow the installation steps carefully, and perform regular checks to ensure long-term reliability. Whether you’re cruising on a V-twin or carving corners on a sportbike, a stable phone mount is a small upgrade that makes a big difference.

Ride safe, and keep your phone—and focus—where they belong.
