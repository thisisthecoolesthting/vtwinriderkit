Privacy

The short version: we collect what we need to make and ship your order.

Free US shipping · ships within 48 business hours · free 30-day returns

What we collect

When you order we collect your name, email, shipping address and phone number (for delivery updates), the items you chose and any text you typed into the personalizer. Card details are entered on Stripe's checkout page and never touch our servers.

Who we share it with

Stripe processes payments. Our print partner (through Printify) receives your name, shipping address, email and the print file so they can make and ship your order. We do not sell your information.

Analytics and cookies

We use Google Analytics 4 to count visits and see which pages work. It sets its own cookies and receives the pages you view. You can block it with a content blocker or your browser settings. Your bag is kept in your own browser.

Your choices

Email hello@vtwinriderkit.org to correct or delete your order information. We keep order records as long as needed for taxes, support and fraud prevention.

Children

This site is for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children.
