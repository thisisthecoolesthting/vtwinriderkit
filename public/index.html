{{FAQ}}

Shipping

Everything is made to order and ships from US print partners to US addresses. Production takes 2 to 5 business days, then 3 to 8 business days in transit. Shipping is Printify's own rate for your items and is shown at checkout before you pay; we do not mark it up. If you need something by a service date, order at least three weeks ahead and email us first.

Returns

Because each item is printed for you, we cannot take back an order for size or a change of mind. If it arrives damaged, misprinted or with text that differs from what you submitted, email us within 30 days with a photo and we will reprint or refund it. Check the spelling in the preview before you pay: what you see is what prints.

Privacy

We collect your name, email, shipping address and the personalization text only to make and ship your order. Payment is handled by Stripe; we never see your card number. Your details go to Stripe and to our print partner and carrier, and nowhere else. We do not sell data. Ad pixels, if enabled, only measure visits and purchases.
