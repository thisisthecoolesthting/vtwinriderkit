Free 30-day returns

You can return any V-Twin Rider Kit order within 30 days of delivery, and return shipping is free. Email hello@vtwinriderkit.org with your order details and we will send return instructions. Refunds go back to your original payment method.

Wrong size? We exchange or reprint it

If the size is not right, we exchange it or reprint it in the right size at no cost to you. You do not pay for the new shipping either.

Personalized pieces

Items with a name or text on them are printed for one person, so you do not have to ship them back: tell us within 30 days and we will refund you or reprint it, whichever you prefer. Please double-check spelling before you order. If we make an error, we fix it.

Damaged, defective or misprinted

Send us a photo and your order number and we will reprint it or refund you, free.

Cancellations and changes

Orders go to production quickly. Email us right away with your order number and we will do what we can before printing starts.
