## The 10 Best Motorcycle Backpacks for Everyday Commuting

When it comes to commuting on your V-twin or sportbike, having the right backpack can make all the difference. A good motorcycle backpack isn’t just about carrying your essentials—it’s about comfort, safety, and functionality. Whether you’re navigating city streets or hitting the highway, these 10 backpacks are designed to meet the needs of everyday riders.

### 1. Kriega R25  
The **Kriega R25** is a favorite among riders for its durability and ergonomic design. Made from 1000D Cordura, it’s waterproof and built to last. The Quadloc harness system distributes weight evenly across your shoulders and chest, making it comfortable for long rides. It also features a roll-top closure, reflective panels for visibility, and a hydration bladder compartment. Perfect for sportbike riders who value sleek design and practicality.

### 2. Ogio Mach 5  
The **Ogio Mach 5** is designed with aerodynamics in mind, making it ideal for sportbike riders who prioritize speed and efficiency. Its wind tunnel-tested design reduces drag, while the padded shoulder straps and back panel ensure comfort. The backpack includes a dedicated laptop sleeve, multiple compartments, and a waterproof pocket. The Mach 5 also features an integrated helmet net for added versatility.

### 3. Alpinestars Tech-Air Backpack  
For riders who prioritize safety, the **Alpinestars Tech-Air Backpack** integrates with the Tech-Air airbag system, providing an extra layer of protection. It’s constructed from abrasion-resistant materials and includes a hydration bladder compartment, reflective details, and a roll-top closure. While it’s more specialized than other options, it’s a great choice for commuters who want peace of mind on the road.

### 4. REV’IT! Stealth 2 Backpack  
The **REV’IT! Stealth 2 Backpack** combines style and functionality with its sleek, minimalist design. It’s made from water-repellent materials and features a roll-top closure for weather resistance. The backpack includes a padded laptop compartment, a front organizer pocket, and adjustable shoulder straps. Its compact size makes it a great option for urban commuters on sportbikes.

### 5. Oxford Aqua T50  
The **Oxford Aqua T50** is a budget-friendly option that doesn’t skimp on features. It’s fully waterproof, thanks to its roll-top design and welded seams. The backpack includes a padded laptop compartment, a front pocket for quick access, and reflective detailing for visibility. Its simple yet effective design makes it a reliable choice for everyday commuting.

### 6. Klim Nac Pak  
The **Klim Nac Pak** is a lightweight, minimalist backpack designed for riders who prefer a streamlined look. It’s made from durable materials and includes a hydration bladder compartment, a small storage pocket, and a helmet net. While it’s smaller than other options, its compact design makes it perfect for short commutes or as a secondary pack for longer rides.

### 7. Tourmaster Select 20  
The **Tourmaster Select 20** offers a balance of storage and comfort. It features a large main compartment, a separate laptop sleeve, and multiple smaller pockets for organization. The backpack is water-resistant and includes padded shoulder straps and a chest strap for added stability. Its generous storage capacity makes it a great choice for commuters who need to carry more gear.

### 8. Nelson-Rigg Hurricane 20  
The **Nelson-Rigg Hurricane 20** is a versatile backpack designed for all-season use. It’s constructed from weather-resistant materials and includes a roll-top closure for added protection. The backpack features a padded laptop sleeve, a front organizer pocket, and reflective accents. Its ergonomic design and adjustable straps make it comfortable for long rides.

### 9. Givi ST602  
The **Givi ST602** is a stylish and functional backpack designed for urban commuters. It’s made from water-repellent materials and includes a roll-top closure, a padded laptop compartment, and multiple pockets for organization. The backpack also features a helmet net and reflective detailing. Its slim profile makes it a great option for riders who want a sleek, modern look.

### 10. Joe Rocket Velocity  
The **Joe Rocket Velocity** is a sporty backpack designed for riders who value performance and style. It’s constructed from durable materials and includes a roll-top closure for weather resistance. The backpack features a padded laptop compartment, a front organizer pocket, and reflective accents. Its aerodynamic design and comfortable fit make it a great choice for sportbike riders.

## What to Look for in a Motorcycle Backpack  

When choosing a motorcycle backpack for everyday commuting, consider the following factors:  

- **Comfort:** Look for padded shoulder straps, a ventilated back panel, and a chest strap to distribute weight evenly.  
- **Weather Resistance:** Waterproof or water-resistant materials, roll-top closures, and welded seams can keep your belongings dry.  
- **Storage:** Consider your storage needs, including compartments for a laptop, hydration bladder, and other essentials.  
- **Visibility:** Reflective panels or accents can improve your visibility on the road, especially during night rides.  
- **Safety:** Some backpacks integrate with airbag systems or include abrasion-resistant materials for added protection.  
- **Fit:** Ensure the backpack fits snugly without restricting movement or interfering with your riding posture.  

By considering these factors, you can find a backpack that meets your needs and enhances your commuting experience. Whether you’re riding a V-twin cruiser or a sportbike, the right backpack can make your daily ride safer, more comfortable, and more enjoyable.
