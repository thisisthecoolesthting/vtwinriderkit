## Choosing and Installing a Universal Motorcycle Windshield

A windshield can significantly improve your riding experience by deflecting wind, rain, and road debris. For riders of cruisers and other motorcycles who find their stock wind protection lacking or non-existent, a universal windshield offers a versatile solution. This guide focuses on a common size: a 16.7-inch universal windscreen, outlining its potential benefits, considerations for choosing one, and a general installation overview.

### Why Consider a Universal Windshield?

Universal windshields are designed to fit a wide range of motorcycles, typically utilizing adjustable mounting hardware. This adaptability makes them an attractive option for owners of various cruiser models, and even some standard bikes, who might not have an OEM (Original Equipment Manufacturer) windshield readily available or prefer a more economical solution.

The primary benefits include:

*   **Wind Protection:** Reduces fatigue on longer rides by deflecting direct wind pressure from your chest and head.
*   **Debris Shield:** Offers a barrier against insects, small stones, and other road detritus.
*   **Weather Protection:** Provides a degree of shelter from rain and cold wind.
*   **Customization:** Allows riders to add wind protection without being limited by specific model-year accessories.

### Key Features of a 16.7-inch Universal Windshield

A 16.7-inch universal windshield typically refers to its height, usually measured from the top of the headlight cut-out to the top edge of the shield, or in some cases, the overall vertical dimension.

Common characteristics include:

*   **Material:** Most are made from durable polycarbonate or acrylic. Polycarbonate generally offers higher impact resistance, while acrylic can be more optically clear and scratch-resistant if properly cared for.
*   **Mounting System:** Universal windshields predominantly use fork-mounted clamps. These are designed to attach to the motorcycle's front forks, often adjustable to accommodate different fork diameters and rake angles.
*   **Adjustability:** Many universal designs allow for angle and height adjustments once mounted, enabling the rider to fine-tune the airflow for their specific height and riding position.
*   **Tint Options:** Available in clear, light tint (smoke), or darker tints, offering choices for aesthetics and glare reduction.

### Is a 16.7-inch Windshield Right for You?

The right windshield height is crucial for optimal performance. A general rule of thumb for cruising and touring is that the top edge of the windshield should be level with, or slightly below, the tip of your nose when you are seated in your normal riding position and looking straight ahead. This allows you to look over the screen comfortably, while still deflecting wind over your head.

A 16.7-inch windshield (approximately 42.4 cm) can be a good starting point for many riders, but its actual effectiveness depends on:

*   **Your Height and Seating Position:** Taller riders or those with an upright seating posture might find it too short, resulting in helmet buffeting. Shorter riders might find it too tall.
*   **Motorcycle Geometry:** The rake of your forks and the position of your handlebars will influence the windshield's angle and perceived height from the rider's perspective.
*   **Desired Coverage:** Consider if you primarily want chest protection or if head protection is also a priority.

It’s often beneficial to measure your current riding height relative to where a windshield would mount on your forks to get a better estimate of how a 16.7-inch screen would position itself.

### Installation Overview for a Universal Windshield

While "universal" implies adaptability, a perfect fit and straightforward installation still require attention to detail. This is a general guide; always refer to the specific instructions provided with your chosen windshield.

**Tools You'll Likely Need:**

*   Hex wrenches or socket set (for mounting bolts)
*   Screwdrivers (if applicable to shield attachment)
*   Measuring tape
*   Marker (optional, for trial fitment)
*   Soft cloth

**General Steps:**

1.  **Unpack and Inspect:** Carefully unbox all components. Ensure all parts are present and free from damage. Lay out the hardware and identify each piece, referencing the included diagram.

2.  **Trial Fit the Brackets (Without the Shield):**
    *   Identify the mounting points on your motorcycle's front forks. These are typically the exposed parts of the fork tubes below the triple tree.
    *   Without attaching the windshield itself, loosely assemble the fork clamps and mounting arms.
    *   Position them on your forks to get an idea of the reach and adjustability. Ensure they clear brake lines, cables, and other components.
    *   Do not over-tighten yet; you'll need room for adjustment.

3.  **Attach the Windshield to the Mounting Arms:**
    *   Many universal shields attach to the mounting arms via bolts and sometimes rubber grommets or washers.
    *   Align the holes in the windshield with the designated points on the mounting arms.
    *   Install the fasteners, but leave them slightly loose initially.

4.  **Mount the Entire Assembly onto the Forks:**
    *   Carefully lift the windshield and mounting arms as an assembly.
    *   Position the fork clamps back onto the forks, aligning them with your earlier trial fitment.
    *   Ensure the windshield is centered on the bike.

5.  **Adjust for Optimal Position:**
    *   **Height and Angle:** Sit on your motorcycle in your normal riding position.
        *   Adjust the height so you can comfortably look over the top edge.
        *   Adjust the angle of the windshield. A more upright angle generally offers more protection but can catch more wind, while a slightly laid-back angle can offer smoother airflow over the top. Experiment to find a balance that reduces buffeting.
    *   **Clearance:** Check that the windshield does not interfere with your handlebars, mirrors, or any other controls when turning the handlebars fully lock-to-lock. This is a critical safety check.
    *   **Stability:** Gently push on the windshield to ensure it feels secure and doesn't wobble excessively.

6.  **Tighten All Fasteners:** Once you are satisfied with the positioning and clearance, systematically tighten all mounting bolts and fasteners. Work in stages, gently tightening opposite bolts to ensure even pressure and prevent stress on the components. Do not overtighten, as this can strip threads or crack plastic components. Refer to any torque specifications in the instructions.

7.  **Final Checks:**
    *   Retest full handlebar movement.
    *   Double-check that all bolts are snug.
    *   Take a short, low-speed ride to assess its performance and make any minor adjustments if needed. Sometimes, what feels right in the garage is different on the road.

### Maintenance Tips

*   **Cleaning:** Use a soft cloth and a specialized plastic cleaner, or mild soap and water. Avoid harsh chemicals, ammonia-based cleaners (like window cleaner), or abrasive cloths, as these can scratch or cloud the plastic.
*   **Inspection:** Periodically check all mounting hardware for tightness. Vibrations from riding can loosen fasteners over time.

A universal 16.7-inch windshield can be a practical upgrade for many motorcycles, offering a blend of protection and aesthetic enhancement. By carefully considering your needs and following proper installation guidelines, you can significantly improve your riding comfort.
