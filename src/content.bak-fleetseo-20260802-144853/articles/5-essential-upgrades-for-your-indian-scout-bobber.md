## 5 Essential Upgrades for Your Indian Scout Bobber  

The Indian Scout Bobber is a sleek, powerful cruiser that blends classic styling with modern performance. While it’s a fantastic bike straight from the factory, a few strategic upgrades can enhance its rideability, comfort, and style. Whether you’re looking to improve handling, boost power, or personalize your ride, here are five essential upgrades to consider.  

### 1. Performance Exhaust System  

**Why Upgrade?**  
The stock exhaust on the Scout Bobber is designed to meet noise and emissions regulations, which often means sacrificing performance and sound. A high-quality aftermarket exhaust can unlock extra horsepower, improve throttle response, and give your bike a deeper, more aggressive tone.  

**What to Look For:**  
- **Material:** Stainless steel or titanium for durability and weight savings.  
- **Design:** Slip-on vs. full system—slip-ons are easier to install, while full systems offer maximum performance gains.  
- **Sound Level:** Some systems are street-legal with baffles, while others are louder for a more aggressive note.  

Popular options include Vance & Hines’ 3" slip-ons or a full system from Freedom Performance, both known for their deep V-twin rumble.  

### 2. Suspension Upgrades  

**Why Upgrade?**  
The Scout Bobber’s suspension is tuned for a stiff, sporty feel, which can be harsh on rough roads or long rides. Upgrading the suspension improves comfort and handling, especially if you frequently ride two-up or carry extra weight.  

**What to Look For:**  
- **Rear Shocks:** Adjustable shocks from brands like Progressive Suspension or Öhlins allow you to fine-tune preload and damping.  
- **Fork Cartridges:** Upgraded internals (like those from Racetech) improve front-end responsiveness.  
- **Ride Height:** Some shocks offer increased travel for better bump absorption.  

A set of Progressive 444 series shocks is a popular choice for balancing comfort and performance without breaking the bank.  

### 3. Comfort Seat  

**Why Upgrade?**  
The stock Bobber seat prioritizes style over long-distance comfort. A well-designed aftermarket seat reduces fatigue and provides better support for extended rides.  

**What to Look For:**  
- **Material:** High-density foam or gel inserts for better cushioning.  
- **Shape:** A slightly wider seat with contoured bolsters improves support.  
- **Height:** Some seats add a bit of lift for taller riders, while others maintain the low-slung Bobber look.  

Brands like Mustang and Corbin offer Scout Bobber-specific seats with improved ergonomics and premium upholstery.  

### 4. High-Performance Air Intake  

**Why Upgrade?**  
Pairing an aftermarket air intake with your new exhaust can further boost horsepower and throttle response by improving airflow into the engine. The stock airbox is restrictive, and an open-style intake lets the engine breathe more freely.  

**What to Look For:**  
- **Filter Type:** Washable, high-flow filters (like K&N) for longevity and performance.  
- **Design:** Some intakes retain a minimalist look, while others add a bold, exposed element.  
- **Tuning Required:** Many intakes require an ECU flash or fuel controller to optimize air/fuel ratios.  

The Indian Stage 1 intake is a popular OEM+ option, but aftermarket choices like the S&S Stealth Air Cleaner offer a more aggressive aesthetic.  

### 5. LED Lighting Upgrades  

**Why Upgrade?**  
The Scout Bobber’s stock lighting is functional but can be improved for visibility and style. LED upgrades provide brighter illumination, a modern look, and longer lifespan compared to halogen bulbs.  

**What to Look For:**  
- **Headlight:** A high-output LED headlight (like JW Speaker or Motodemic) improves nighttime visibility.  
- **Turn Signals:** Smoked or clear LED signals maintain the Bobber’s clean lines.  
- **Taillight:** Integrated LED taillights can streamline the rear end while enhancing brake visibility.  

Many riders opt for Indian’s own accessory LED headlight or aftermarket options from companies like Custom Dynamics for plug-and-play upgrades.  

### What to Look for in Scout Bobber Upgrades  

When choosing upgrades, consider:  
- **Compatibility:** Ensure parts are designed specifically for the Scout Bobber (2018+).  
- **Installation Difficulty:** Some mods (like exhausts) are DIY-friendly, while others (ECU tuning) may require professional help.  
- **Legal Compliance:** Check local laws regarding exhaust noise and lighting modifications.  
- **Warranty Impact:** Some upgrades (especially engine-related) may affect your factory warranty.  

By focusing on these key areas, you can tailor your Scout Bobber to your riding style while maintaining its iconic look and performance. Whether you prioritize power, comfort, or aesthetics, these upgrades will help you get the most out of your ride.
