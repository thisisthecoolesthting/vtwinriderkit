## Sportbike Number Plate Holders and Fender Eliminators: Clean Up Your Tail  

A sleek, aggressive tail section is a hallmark of sportbike styling, but bulky stock fenders and license plate holders can ruin the look. Fortunately, **number plate holders** and **fender eliminators** are popular aftermarket upgrades that streamline your bike’s rear end while maintaining legality. Whether you ride a V-twin cruiser or a high-performance sportbike, these mods can enhance aesthetics and functionality.  

### Why Upgrade Your Stock Fender?  

Most factory-installed fenders are designed with practicality—not style—in mind. They often extend far beyond the rear tire, housing oversized turn signals, reflectors, and a bulky license plate bracket. While functional, they add unnecessary weight and disrupt the bike’s lines.  

A **fender eliminator kit** replaces the stock setup with a minimalist design, tucking the license plate closer to the tail and often integrating sleeker lighting. Meanwhile, a **number plate holder** (or license plate relocation kit) moves the plate to a more discreet position, such as under the seat or beside the rear wheel.  

### Types of Fender Eliminators  

#### 1. **Bolt-On Eliminator Kits**  
The most common type, these kits replace the stock fender with a simplified bracket that holds the license plate and often includes mounts for LED turn signals. They’re designed for easy installation, requiring minimal modification.  

- **Pros:** Affordable, no cutting required, retains functionality.  
- **Cons:** May still leave some wiring exposed if not integrated with a tail tidy.  

#### 2. **Integrated Tail Tidy Kits**  
These kits combine a fender eliminator with a **relocated license plate mount** and often include built-in LED lighting (brake lights and turn signals). They provide the cleanest look by hiding wiring and mounting the plate closer to the undertail.  

- **Pros:** Ultra-sleek, improved lighting options, better wire management.  
- **Cons:** More expensive, may require minor wiring work.  

#### 3. **Side-Mounted License Plate Relocators**  
Popular among cruisers and some sportbikes, these kits move the plate to the side of the rear wheel, often attached to the swingarm or axle. They’re favored by riders who want a custom, asymmetrical look.  

- **Pros:** Unique styling, keeps plate visible (important for legality).  
- **Cons:** Can interfere with chain maintenance, may not suit all bike styles.  

### Key Features to Consider  

When shopping for a fender eliminator or number plate holder, keep these factors in mind:  

- **Material:** Most are made from **aluminum** or **stainless steel** for durability. Avoid cheap plastic brackets that may crack over time.  
- **Lighting Compatibility:** Ensure the kit supports your bike’s lighting setup—some require LED conversions.  
- **Legal Compliance:** Check local laws—some states require the plate to be illuminated or positioned at a specific angle.  
- **Installation Difficulty:** Bolt-on kits are beginner-friendly; integrated kits may need wiring adjustments.  

### What to Look for in a Quality Kit  

1. **Durable Construction** – Look for powder-coated or anodized metal to resist rust and wear.  
2. **Plug-and-Play Wiring** – Kits with OEM-style connectors make installation easier.  
3. **Adjustable Mounting** – Some kits allow you to tweak the plate angle for a custom fit.  
4. **Included Hardware** – High-quality stainless steel bolts and spacers prevent corrosion.  

### Installation Tips  

While most kits are straightforward to install, here are a few pointers:  

- **Disconnect the Battery** before working with wiring to avoid shorts.  
- **Use Thread Locker** on bolts to prevent vibrations from loosening them.  
- **Check Clearances** – Ensure the plate won’t rub against the tire or exhaust.  

### Final Thoughts  

Upgrading to a fender eliminator or number plate holder is one of the easiest ways to sharpen your bike’s appearance. Whether you opt for a simple bolt-on or a full integrated tail tidy, the result is a cleaner, more aggressive rear end that complements your ride’s performance-oriented design. Just be sure to choose a kit that meets legal requirements and suits your bike’s specific make and model.  

Ready to ditch the bulky fender? Browse kits designed for your motorcycle, and enjoy the instant visual upgrade.
